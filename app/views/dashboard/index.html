  • Tackling HIV Stigma and Discrimination

    Type: elearning
    The aim of this module is to explore the important link between HIV-related stigma and public health outcomes, such as quality of care.The healthcare sector is one of the main settings where people living with HIV and those perceived to be living with, or at risk of, HIV, experience stigma and discrimination.Stigma and health inequalities create significant barriers to accessing testing, prevention and care. This has become more acute since COVID-19 and without action, we risk progress slipping further. Everyone involved in the health and social care sector has a responsibility to stop HIV stigma and address health inequalities throughout their work.Testing your perceptions and updating your HIV knowledge as well as exploring practical approaches which can be easily implemented, within your role, service and across the healthcare system, is key to ensuring we all have access to the same healthcare.

  • Optimising the Expression of Breastmilk and the Importance of Flange Sizing

    Type: Video - 16 min 58 sec
    Funded by NHSE SW, Health Innovation Wessex is supporting the spread of a quality improvement project initiated in Southampton. The project focuses on equity of access to appropriate equipment for expressing maternal breastmilk.There is published research to support correctly measured and fitted flanges on breast pumps. Correctly fitted flangesincrease yield of breastmilk, reduce mother’s discomfort and can reduce the incidence of developing mastitis. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/08903344241296036Currently there is low level of knowledge and nipple measurement is not standard practice. Trusts usually only stock a standard size which is far too large for the majority. Mothers are required to buy the correct fitting flange, which disadvantages those from low income families.This webinar supports clinicians implement the best practice of nipple measurement and correct flange fitting for mother expressing breastmilk.As part of a regional quality improvement project Health Innovation Wessex has supported University Hospitals Southampton in the development of a patient information video on the topic. A full toolkit of resources has been developed by HealthInnovation Wessex to supportthe spread of this practice.https://healthinnovationwessex.org.uk/projects/658/early-breast-milk-improvement-project-toolkitor contact holly.green@hiwessex.net

  • COVID-19 Vaccination

    This elearning programme provides information about the COVID-19 national vaccination programme. This programme began in the UK in December 2020 to protect against serious illness and death from the SARS-CoV-2 virus. It is designed to provide healthcare practitioners involved in delivering the national COVID-19 vaccination programme with the knowledge they need to safely and effectively deliver the programme and to promote high uptake of the vaccine. All those undertaking this elearning programme should complete the core knowledge session as this is designed to provide essential knowledge about COVID-19 and the key principles of immunisation needed to deliver the vaccines. Learners should then complete the vaccine-specific knowledge sessions for the vaccine(s) they will be involved in giving as these provide more detailed information including how the vaccine works, how it should be stored, prepared and administered and any contraindications, precautions and potential vaccine reactions. The assessment sessions should be completed after each knowledge session. The information presented was correct at the time of publication. As the COVID-19 vaccination programme is constantly changing to ensure optimal protection to those who need it, some of the information may change. Updates are being made to this programme as new information becomes available.

  • Prescribing Implementation Toolkits

    Prescribing Implementation Toolkits support the development of prescribing by a range of health professionals at post-professional registration level. The 'Preparing to Prescribe' toolkit offers advice, information and signposting to resources to support those planning to undertake a prescribing qualification in the UK. Toolkits are aimed at healthcare professionals, managers/commissioners, and others with an interest in prescribing. The ‘Preparing to Prescribe’ toolkit was developed at the University of Surrey, based on research evidence and multi-professional input from experts. Further resources are planned, informed by research led by Professor Nicola Carey (University of the Highlands and Islands) and Dr Karen Stenner (University of Surrey), in collaboration with researchers, patient representatives and expert advisors.
