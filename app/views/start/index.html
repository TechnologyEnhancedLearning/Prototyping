A place to learn and grow

The Learning Hub supports organisations and individuals across the NHS health and social care workforce, including local government and universities.

Who is the Learning Hub for?

Learners can find lots of interesting resources to support learning and professional development locally, regionally and nationally.

Organisations registered on the Learning Hub can contribute and share a wide variety of self‑managed content, knowledge and learning resources.

Keep up to date

For more information about the Learning Hub visit the Technology Enhanced Learning (TEL) blog.

Follow us on Twitter @HEE_TEL

Contribute and Share

Share your knowledge, insights and expertise by contributing elearning, video, audio, images, web links, documents and articles on a range of subjects.
